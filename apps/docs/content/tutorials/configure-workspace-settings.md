Give your workspace a clear name so your team can recognise it. This tutorial shows how to rename your workspace.

## Before you begin

- You need permission to change workspace settings.

## Steps

1. On the **Dashboard**, click **Settings**.
2. Click **General**.
3. In **Workspace name**, enter `Acme Docs Workspace`.
4. Click **Save changes**.

## Expected outcome

- The message `Workspace settings saved.` appears.
- The **Workspace name** field shows `Acme Docs Workspace`.

> **Tip:** You can change the workspace name again at any time.
