Projects help your team keep track of a piece of work. This tutorial shows how to add a new project to your workspace.

## Before you begin

- You need access to an AcmeDesk workspace.

## Steps

1. On the **Dashboard**, click **Projects** in the sidebar.
2. Click **New project**.
3. In **Project name**, enter `Docs Demo Project`.
4. Click **Create project**.

## Expected outcome

- The message `Project "Docs Demo Project" created.` appears.
- **Docs Demo Project** is listed in the projects table.

> **Note:** Project names must be unique within a workspace.
