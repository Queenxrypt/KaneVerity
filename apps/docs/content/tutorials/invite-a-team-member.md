Invite colleagues so they can work in your workspace. Each person gets a role that controls what they can do.

## Before you begin

- You need an email address for the person you are inviting.

## Steps

1. On the **Dashboard**, click **Team Members** in the sidebar.
2. Click **Invite member**.
3. In **Email address**, enter `new.teammate@example.com`.
4. In **Role**, select **Member**.
5. Click **Send invite**.

## Expected outcome

- The message `Invitation sent to new.teammate@example.com as Member.` appears.
- The new member is listed in the team members table with the status **Invited**.

> **Note:** In this demo workspace, invitations are simulated and no email is sent.
