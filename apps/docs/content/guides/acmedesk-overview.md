AcmeDesk is a workspace for small teams. It brings your projects, the people you work with and your integrations together in one place.

## Key concepts

- **Workspace:** the shared space for your team. Each workspace has a name and a timezone.
- **Projects:** the pieces of work your team is tracking, each with a status and an owner.
- **Team members:** the people who can access the workspace, each with a role of Admin, Member or Viewer.
- **API keys:** credentials that let your own scripts and services work with AcmeDesk.

## Roles

Admins can change workspace settings and invite people. Members can create and update projects. Viewers have read-only access.
